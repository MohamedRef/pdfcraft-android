PdfCraft's text recognition (Scan & OCR) uses the two text models, unmodified. Its scanner finds
the page in a photo with scan-edges.onnx, turns it the right way up with scan-upright.onnx and,
when asked to, flattens a curled page with scan-flatten.onnx.
