# PdfCraft Android 0.0.25-beta (25012)

## New features

- **Document scanner, rebuilt in-app (testing):** an in-app camera with the page's outline and advice in the viewfinder, auto-capture, and book mode with a spine guide; the phone's own camera app stays available. Pages are found by a detector network and refined on the full-size photo, kept at their true proportions, turned the right way up, and can be cropped with a magnifier and snapping to the paper's edge. Clean scan gains document types that shape the page's tone, with gentle sharpening of photos and Flatten or Grayscale suggestions, a look per page with a strength slider, a looks strip with compare and apply to all, paper choice and a Whiteboard look, 1-bit black and white, grain smoothing and print sharpening. Flatten can straighten a curled page on request, page by page, from its lines of text when the network gives nothing better, and trims the flattened page's border. Book mode splits one photo of an open book into two pages. Kept scans report moiré, fingers, glare and stains (detection only). Everything runs on the phone, with no Google services.
- **Study tools:** flashcards with FSRS spaced repetition, cloze cards and Anki export; masking tape you tap to reveal, a Tape layer and quiz mode over highlights; a focus timer, reading-time stats and a Continue studying card; lecture recording synced to the comments written; study-note export as Markdown, CSV or PDF summary.
- **Viewer:** document tabs and split view with copy across and back-links, two pages side by side on wide windows, presentation mode with a laser pointer, external display, reading colours, a search results list, a layers panel, hiding annotations, and sharing or printing without them.
- **Editing and pages:** select several objects; grouped text with document fonts, spacing and an overflow warning; crop, flip, rotate, arrange and replace pictures; duplicate, replace, reverse and split pages, and page labels; headers, footers, watermarks and backgrounds with a preview; note margins that grow pages; blank notebooks with paper templates.
- **Ink and drawing:** a pen per colour, width and opacity; unlimited favourite pens; a colour wheel and colour-blind-safe palettes; ruler, protractor, smart pen gestures, zoom writing box, circle-to-select, S Pen button actions and experimental low-latency ink.
- **Documents and tools:** prepare forms (detect, place, edit and order fields); mark, review and apply redactions, then check for hidden data; compare with an older version; PDF/A check and fix and print readiness; accessibility check, alternate text and auto-tagging; read handwriting into ink comments' text; distance, perimeter and area measuring with CSV export; certify and invisible signatures; edit metadata, bookmarks and attachments; links to another PDF's page or bookmark; versioned backups to a chosen folder; collections, tags, colour labels and search across documents; advanced compression and more export formats.
- **Diagnostics log:** a detailed, shareable log from the phone covering the viewer, tools, pens, scanner and performance.

## Fixes

- **Scanner fixes from review:** the strength slider now changes the page in Clean scan with document types on (#424); a stricter book-page gutter rule, with page measurements kept with the straightened page (#427); low-light merge of four photos is off by default, because it could lose the photos and the crop (#426); barcodes, stamps and soft print are no longer taken for photographs; automatic flattening keeps a flattened page only when at least 8 lines were followed.
- **Ink:** accurate pen taps, ink at the page edge, smooth fast finger strokes, per-stroke undo, and ink kept until the engine accepts it.
- **Saving and safety:** the right recovery copy is kept and write-backs are verified; engine panics become errors instead of crashes; temporary cache files are cleaned and unused file grants released.
- **Updater, language and accessibility:** translated update errors, downloads that survive leaving About, every missing string translated, Undo and Redo in the app's language, and TalkBack labels, 48 dp touch targets and right-to-left seek taps.

## Improvements

- **Engine:** the Rust engine is synced with upstream v0.21.1.
- **Speed and memory:** annotations are parsed once per refresh after an edit, unchanged comment appearances are kept, page pixels reach the app in one copy, separate engine queues bound the viewer's memory, tiles the view no longer needs are not drawn, and the display copy is drawn beside inspection.

## Known limits and testing

- **Nothing in this release has run on a phone.** The scanner is new and meant for testing with the options and kept scans in `docs/ANDROID_SCAN_TESTING.md`; its figures come from drawn test photos and the owner's reference photos, not from device use.
- Automatic flattening stays off by default. Low-light merge is off by default.
- Large-file opening timings (#211, #212, #236) and background-save timing (#97) wait on the diagnostics log from this build.
- Automated Rust/provenance, Android JVM and signed ARM64 release checks must pass before publishing. Not verified on a physical device.

## Installing

After the source, test, release-signing, ABI, manifest and checksum gates succeed, the in-app updater offers `pdfcraft-android-0.0.25-beta.apk` (arm64-v8a) from the official PdfCraft Android fork's GitHub Release. You can also download it there. Check that the SHA-256 and the manifest version `0.0.25-beta (25012)` match, then approve the installation in Android. It installs over 0.0.24-beta (24012) with the same package and signing certificate; earlier releases are not replaced.
