# PdfCraft Android 0.0.47-beta (47012)

This release holds the scanner work that followed the owner's scan of 21 November on 0.0.46-beta (46012): what happens after Done, the camera's buttons, checks on the page outline and on auto-capture, a safer Save, and less waiting while pages are made.

Everything here was measured on a computer, not on a phone: on drawn pages, on his diagnostics log (numbers only) and on his kept scan of 21 November.

## New features

- **A finished scan opens as continuous pages.** After Done the pages are shown one under the other, each as wide as the screen, with "Page n of m". Organize pages in the top bar shows the grid as before, and View pages comes back to the same page (#796).
- **Review pages, Undo and Done are separate buttons on the camera.** (#797)
- **Back to the camera in one tap from Review pages.** (#799)
- **Pages whose borders are unsure are offered for a check before the scan is saved.** (#798)
- **A page shows at once in a quick first picture while it is made.** On a computer the first picture of his 12 photos took 110 ms at the median, against 2.9 s for the page itself. The saved page is unchanged (#793).
- **The tab strip stays in view with one document, and a PDF can be chosen from inside one.** (#789)

## Fixes

- **Save keeps the scan until the place its PDF goes to has it.** Before, the scan was cleared before the PDF was written, so a refused write could lose it. Now a failed save says so and the pages are still there (#788).
- **A page outline whose corners cannot be one sheet is refused.** On the owner's 11 pile photos of 19 November, wrong outlines fell from 3 to 0. Those 3 photos now say that no page was found, and the 8 right ones stay right. Finding the top sheet of a pile is still open (#795).
- **Auto-capture and focus: eight faults in the capture logic are fixed.** On 21 November auto-capture took 0 of 10 photos. The limits themselves are unchanged until a phone run shows what they should be (#800).
- **The camera screen no longer passes touches to the screen under it.** (#791)
- **A finger on the page is filled whole, or left.** (#792)
- **Clean scan keeps the shadows of a photograph on the page.** Measured on drawn pages whose marks are known (#801).

## Improvements

- **Flattening that could not be kept is not run.** On his 12 kept pages the flattening network ran on 10 instead of 12, and the pages came out identical (#803).
- **The page search looks at a slanted page at its own angle first.** On his 12 kept photos the search made 57 looks instead of 65 and found the same page on all 12 (#804).
- **A saved PDF is checked against the reviewed pages by a test:** page count, order, size on paper and pixels (#802).
- **The interface is in English and Arabic only.** (#790)
- **Diagnostics log.** New fields, as counts and words only, never a file name or a page's content, for auto-capture's phases, focus, the sheet check, the first picture, the continuous pages, and the two speed-ups above.
- **A measuring protocol for the scanner on a phone** is in `docs/ANDROID_SCAN_BASELINE.md` and `docs/ANDROID_SCAN_AUTO.md` (#794, #800).

## Known limits and testing

- **None of this release's changes has been tried on a phone or tablet.** The Android side was compiled and tested by the automated checks only. Issues #779 to #786 stay open until a phone run.
- **Unverified:**
  - whether auto-capture now fires, and whether its limits fit a real room;
  - how the continuous pages scroll and use memory with 20 pages, in either orientation, right to left, and with TalkBack;
  - how much time the first picture and the two speed-ups save on a phone;
  - whether the new camera buttons are easy to reach.
- **A pile of sheets:** a wrong outline is refused, but the top sheet is not found yet (#782).
- **A search with no sure look still makes seven looks.** That was 7 of his 12 kept photos (#785).
- **In continuous pages a page is drawn from its 1280 px preview,** so small print can look softer there than in the saved PDF.
- Every scanner change has its own switch under Settings › Diagnostics › Scanner testing, so you can compare it with the old behaviour.
- Source: `main` at the release commit. The automated Rust/provenance, Android JVM and signed ARM64 release checks must pass before publishing.

## Installing

Once the source, test, release-signing, ABI, manifest and checksum gates succeed, the in-app updater offers `pdfcraft-android-0.0.47-beta.apk` (arm64-v8a) from the GitHub Release of the official PdfCraft Android fork. You can also download it there.

Check that the SHA-256 and the manifest version `0.0.47-beta (47012)` match, then approve the installation in Android. It installs over 0.0.46-beta (46012) with the same package and signing certificate.
