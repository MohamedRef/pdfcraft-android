# PdfCraft Android 0.0.44-beta (44012)

This release fixes what testing 0.0.43 on the owner's tablet and phone found. Most of it is for tablets and large books.

## New features

- **Save now, from the title or the menu:** "Unsaved changes" under the title is a Save button, and ⋮ › File starts with **Save**. While it saves, the title says "Saving…" (#190).
- **Progress and Cancel for two more jobs:** Contents from headings and Analyze text styles count the pages they read, and Cancel stops them (#652, #723).

## Fixes

- **Save a copy** leaves the original as it was opened, even when an earlier save of the same visit was waiting for the document to close (#724).
- **Crash recovery keeps lasso edits:** strokes not yet saved now come back after a crash as they were moved, resized, turned, recoloured or deleted with the lasso, or after a scratch-out, not as they were first drawn. If an edit can't be followed (an eraser cut, Undo), the message after the crash says changes made afterwards may be missing (#703).
- **Scan from photos:** discarding the only picked photo on Adjust borders ends the scan instead of opening the camera, and Scan from photos keeps opening the picker afterwards. Before, it did nothing until the app was restarted (#716). Back on a picked photo now says it stays in your gallery, not that it will be deleted (#737).
- **Comments list:** a highlight's preview shows only the highlighted words, not the rest of the line beside them (#735).
- **Draw:** the lasso stays in view on a 384 dp bar with six pens (#697). Every caption on the Draw bar and its sheets is shown whole, in every language (#704). The lasso's turn handle stays out from under the tab strip (#709). The whole-stroke eraser hides each stroke it touches while it is down (#655).
- **Shapes:** a solid's edges stay straight once it is saved (#701).
- **Viewer:** a short file name shows in full on the top bar (#706), and Export's caption stays on one line (#708).
- **Opening and Recent:** a file keeps its access while it is still opening (#705), and a file shows once in Recent and Search, beside an old address that can't open any more (#712).
- **Tablets:** every tool's name is shown whole in Tools (#721), the page beside a sheet is dimmed less (#719), and the app says "this device", not "this phone" (#720).

## Improvements

- **Saving a large book:** after a save that only appends, the document is no longer read again in full (#595). On a 350 MB, 869-page test book on the tablet, that step went from 18–21 ms to 3 ms. The whole save there took 0.54 s, most of it rewriting the 350 MB working copy. It is not yet measured on the owner's book, where the full re-read cost more.
- **Diagnostics log:** `doc_save_stages` says how long each stage of a save took (#595). `ink_journal` says when an edit couldn't be followed (`touched`, #703). A failed camera, picker or shared-pictures request is logged and no longer stops the next one (#716). Counts and times only.

## Known limits and testing

- **Tried on the owner's tablet** (Galaxy Tab S11, SM-X730, Android 17) with a debug build of `main`, on a synthetic 869-page, 350 MB test book and synthetic scan pages, never the owner's files:
  - Scan from photos after a discard (#716);
  - crash recovery after a lasso move and a quarter turn (#703);
  - Cancel in Export to images, stopped at page 102 of 869 with nothing saved (#627);
  - Save in ⋮, "Saving…" during a save, and Save a copy of a writable original that left the original unchanged (#190);
  - the highlight preview (#735);
  - the save timings above (#595).
- **Not tried on the phone** yet, and not with the S Pen.
- **Found on the tablet and not fixed in this release:**
  - a finger highlight starts and ends mid-word (#739);
  - the Comments header counts drawings as threads (#740);
  - a selected highlight's colour bar stays on screen in Present and Read aloud (#741);
  - Present keeps the side rail, and a swipe doesn't turn the page (#742).
- The scanner's features are built and tested on synthetic pages; their acceptance waits for the owner's scans of real documents (#286).
- Source: `main` at the release commit. Automated Rust/provenance, Android JVM and signed ARM64 release checks must pass before publishing.

## Installing

After the source, test, release-signing, ABI, manifest and checksum gates succeed, the in-app updater offers `pdfcraft-android-0.0.44-beta.apk` (arm64-v8a) from the official PdfCraft Android fork's GitHub Release. You can also download it there. Check that the SHA-256 and the manifest version `0.0.44-beta (44012)` match, then approve the installation in Android. It installs over 0.0.43-beta (43012) with the same package and signing certificate.
