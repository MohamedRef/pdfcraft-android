# PdfCraft Android 0.0.45-beta (45012)

This release fixes what the owner's scan of 19 November found: 12 photos in Many pages, of one printed page and of a pile of sheets. Everything here was measured on his log and kept scan on a computer, not on a phone.

## New features

- **Pages the scanner is unsure of are offered for a check:** after any capture or import, the document screen says "N pages may have the wrong borders" with **Check**, for the pages whose page wasn't found or was found with a low confidence, and whose borders nobody has looked at. Check opens Adjust borders on those pages only, one after another. On the 19 November scan it would have offered 7 pages: the 2 saved as whole photos, the 2 saved as ID cards, and 3 others, one of which was right (#758).
- **One paper size for a scan's pages:** a page within 10 % of the paper most of the scan's other pages came out at takes that paper. On the 19 November scan, the page that came out Letter and the one stretched to 1.51 would both have been A4 (#759).

## Fixes

- **A part of a page is no longer saved as an ID card:** a page is matched to ID card only when its outline was found with confidence, or set by hand. On the 19 November scan, two parts of a pile came out card-sized (#759).
- **A scan opened again keeps its pages upright:** after the app had been closed and opened again, a scan reopened from its PDF or from Recent scans was made without the scanner's models. Its pages lost their automatic turn (5 of 12 came back unturned) and flattening (#764).
- **The outline in the camera finds a page lying at a slant:** while it finds nothing, each frame is looked at another way round (30°, 60° or 90° either side), and the way that finds the page is kept. On frames made from the 19 November photos, the outline would have been there on 11 of 12, against 3 before. Auto-capture waits for that outline, so it should now fire too (#295).

## Improvements

- **Save PDF and Share make the pages not ready yet two at once.** On the 19 November scan, Save made 7 pages one after another and took 19.9 s. How much this saves is not measured: part of each page's work already uses several threads (#549).
- **The code behind Draw and the scanner is split into smaller files** (#730). Nothing should change in the app.
- **Diagnostics log:** `unsure_offer`, `paper_hint`, `card_paper`, `paper_size`, `save_lanes`, and the outline's `angle` and `found_turned`. Counts and words only, never a file name or a page's content.

## Known limits and testing

- **None of this release's changes has been tried on a phone or tablet.** The measurements above were made on a computer, on the owner's log and kept scan of 19 November (numbers only; nothing of it is in the repository) and on drawn test pages.
- **A pile of overlapping sheets is still found badly.** The page detector takes a corner of a sheet underneath, or finds nothing. Such pages are now offered for a check (above), but the borders still have to be set by hand (#290).
- **A finger over printed text stays on the page.** Removing it would remove the text under it too, so the scanner says "A finger hid some print. Retake to get it back" (#306).
- Every scanner change has its own switch under Settings › About › Diagnostics, to compare against the old behaviour.
- Source: `main` at the release commit. Automated Rust/provenance, Android JVM and signed ARM64 release checks must pass before publishing.

## Installing

After the source, test, release-signing, ABI, manifest and checksum gates succeed, the in-app updater offers `pdfcraft-android-0.0.45-beta.apk` (arm64-v8a) from the official PdfCraft Android fork's GitHub Release. You can also download it there. Check that the SHA-256 and the manifest version `0.0.45-beta (45012)` match, then approve the installation in Android. It installs over 0.0.44-beta (44012) with the same package and signing certificate.
