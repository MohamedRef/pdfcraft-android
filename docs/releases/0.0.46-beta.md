# PdfCraft Android 0.0.46-beta (46012)

This release fixes four problems from the owner's scan of 20 November on 0.0.45-beta (45012):
- auto-capture never fired;
- the snap on Adjust borders was too strong;
- the page detector missed pages;
- a new look made pages flatten again.

Everything here was measured on a computer, not on a phone: on his diagnostics log (numbers only) and on his kept photos of 19 November.

## New features

- **The line detector looks too when the network is unsure.** When the page detector finds nothing, or finds an unsure page with at most two of its sides on edges of the photo, the engine's line detector is asked as well. Its page is taken when enough of its sides lie on edges. Such pages are still offered for a check (#290).
  - On the 20 November scan, the line detector found 2 of the 3 pages the network missed.
  - On the 19 November pile of sheets, it found the top sheet on two photos: one where the network found nothing, and one where the network's page cut across sheets.

## Fixes

- **Auto-capture fires in a dimmer room and on a less sure outline.** On 20 November auto-capture was on for 11 photos and took none of them, because the advice was never *Ready*. *More light* now starts under a mean brightness of 40 instead of 55; his room sat at 45–72. The outline counts as steady from a confidence of 0.5 instead of 0.6 (#296).
- **A gentler snap on Adjust borders.** A dragged corner or side is caught from 12 dp instead of 20, and let go at 20 dp instead of 32. When you lift your finger, only a handle that is on an edge snaps again; one you pulled free stays where you put it. On 20 November he pulled handles free 3 times on one page, and turned Snap off on 6 of 10 pages (#773).

## Improvements

- **A page made again keeps its flattening.** Each page's flattening decision is now kept until the scan ends, not only those of the last four previews. A new look, or coming back to the app, no longer flattens the pages again. On 20 November a new look on 12 pages flattened 8 of them again, 0.4–1.0 s each. How much this shortens Share and Save on the phone is not measured (#549).
- **Diagnostics log.** New fields, as counts and words only, never a file name or a page's content:
  - `live_hints` when the camera closes: how long each piece of advice showed, and what held auto-capture back;
  - `snaps_free` and the snap's distances on `crop`;
  - `second_look`, `network_sides` and `lines_sides` on `photo`;
  - `flatten_keep_all` on `page`.

## Known limits and testing

- **None of this release's changes has been tried on a phone or tablet.** The measurements above were made on a computer, on the owner's log of 20 November and his kept photos of 19 November. Only numbers are used; nothing of them is in the repository.
- **Unverified:**
  - whether auto-capture now fires;
  - whether a photo taken in a dimmer room is sharp enough;
  - whether 12 and 20 dp feel right for the snap.
- **The line detector can take a wrong page where the network was unsure.** Such a page is still offered for a check. How often it happens is for the next scans to show.
- **In Many pages, the outline in the camera updates two to three times slower while the earlier photos are worked on.** A frame takes a median of 88 ms when nothing else runs, 204 ms while a page is made, and 291 ms while a photo is searched. This release doesn't change that (#295).
- Every scanner change has its own switch under Settings › Diagnostics › Scanner testing, so you can compare it with the old behaviour.
- Source: `main` at the release commit. The automated Rust/provenance, Android JVM and signed ARM64 release checks must pass before publishing.

## Installing

Once the source, test, release-signing, ABI, manifest and checksum gates succeed, the in-app updater offers `pdfcraft-android-0.0.46-beta.apk` (arm64-v8a) from the GitHub Release of the official PdfCraft Android fork. You can also download it there.

Check that the SHA-256 and the manifest version `0.0.46-beta (46012)` match, then approve the installation in Android. It installs over 0.0.45-beta (45012) with the same package and signing certificate.
