# New strings for the scan preview (K04)

Format: `name | English text`. Existing strings reused by the new Kotlin files are listed last, with the reason.

scan_look_color | Color
scan_look_black_white | Black and white
scan_look_strength | Strength
scan_look_strength_percent | %1$d percent
scan_look_paper | Paper
scan_look_paper_white | White
scan_look_paper_keep | Keep color
scan_look_flatten_auto | Auto
scan_look_flatten_on | On
scan_look_flatten_off | Off
scan_look_rotate | Rotate
scan_look_apply_all | Apply to all
scan_look_apply_all_title | Apply to all pages?
scan_look_apply_all_body | Other pages have different looks. Give every page this page's look, strength, paper and flatten setting?
scan_look_apply_confirm | Apply
scan_look_reset | Reset look
scan_preview_description | Page preview
scan_preview_hold_hint | Hold to see the original

## Existing strings reused (not new)

- `tools_filter_original` (Original): the Original look.
- `tools_filter_clean_scan` (Clean scan): the Auto look. Its name is "Clean scan", as S15 asks.
- `tools_filter_grayscale` (Grayscale): the Greyscale look.
- `tools_filter_whiteboard` (Whiteboard): the Whiteboard look.
- `tools_scan_flatten` (Flatten page): the flatten row's label.

Not reused: `tools_filter_auto_colour` reads "Auto color", which is not the Color look (no Auto step in it). `tools_filter_light_text` reads "Light text", not Black and white.
